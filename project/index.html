Welcome to Royal Pearl International School

Royal Pearl International School is a private educational institution located at 66 Davis Street, Anaji in the Sekondi-Takoradi Metropolis of the Western Region, Ghana. The school serves the local community by providing comprehensive primary and basic education. Guided by our motto, "Seeking the Ultimate," we are committed to excellence in everything we do from the classroom to extracurricular activities. Our dedicated staff work closely with families to create a supportive environment where every child can thrive. Since 1998, the school has grown steadily while staying true to its founding motto, "Seeking the Ultimate," building a reputation for academic excellence and strong community values.

Why Choose Us

  • Small class sizes for personalized attention
  • Experienced, qualified teaching staff
  • Strong foundation in primary and basic education
