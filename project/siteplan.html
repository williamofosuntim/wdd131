Royal Pearl International School - Site Plan

WDD 131 - William Ofosu Ntim

Site Name

Royal Pearl International School

I chose this name because it is my actual project school located in Anaji, Sekondi-Takoradi, Ghana. It represents a private Basic and JHS school founded in 1998 with motto "Seeking the Ultimate" and known for academic excellence. The pearl represents the value of each child.

Optional domain: royalpearlschool.edu.gh

Logo: images/school-logo.jpeg (crown over pearl)

Site Purpose

The website provides information about Royal Pearl International School's academic programs, admission process, facilities, staff, and school activities. The site will include About Us with Mission, Vision, Values, History, Facilities, Staff profiles, Activities like Speech and Prize-Giving Day, Prefect induction, AU Day and Heritage Day celebrations, photo gallery, and contact inquiry form for parents.

Scenarios

  • What are the admission requirements and fees for enrolling my child in Basic 1 at Royal Pearl International School in Anaji?
  • Where can I find information about your facilities, Speech and Prize-Giving Day events, and how to contact the administration for a school tour?

Color Schema

  • Primary - Royal Blue #0038A8: Used for header background, footer background, all h2 and h3 headings, and navigation accents.
  • Secondary - White #FFFFFF: Used for main body background and text on blue background.
  • Accent - Light Blue #E6F0FF: Used for section backgrounds and card backgrounds to maintain Blue and White theme.
