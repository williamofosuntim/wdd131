Admissions

Welcome to the admissions page of Royal Pearl International School. We are delighted that you are considering our school for your child's education. Our admissions process is designed to ensure that we select students who will thrive in our academic environment.

Application Process

To apply for admission, please contact the admissions office to request an application form, then submit it with the required documents, including academic records, birth certificate, and any other relevant information. Our admissions team will review your application and contact you for further steps.

Admission Requirements

Applicants must meet the following requirements: age eligibility, academic performance, and a commitment to our school's values and mission. We also consider extracurricular involvement and personal qualities during the admissions process.

Tuition and Fees

Please contact the admissions office for detailed information on tuition and fees associated with enrollment.

Contact Admissions

If you have any questions about the admissions process or would like to schedule a visit, please contact our admissions office at admissions@royalpearl.edu.gh.
